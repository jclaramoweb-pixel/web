Born on this water

Capt. Tanner Martin was born and raised on the Nature Coast, and the creeks, oyster bars and grass flats between Crystal River and Homosassa are the water he learned to fish on. He is the owner and operator of Florida’s On The Fly Charters and the captain on every trip the company runs.

After playing Division I baseball at Stetson University he had a decision to make: the corporate world or the guiding career he had already started part time during college. It was not a hard call. He went all in on showing people a good day in the outdoors, and has guided full time out of Crystal River since 2018.

He specializes in inshore fly fishing and light-tackle spin fishing for redfish, trout and snook, the Homosassa tarpon migration every spring, scalloping in summer and guided duck hunts in winter. Kids, first-timers and seasoned fly anglers all fish with him, and the trip is built around whoever is on the boat.

How Tanner runs a trip

“I tell all my clients I try to make the trip about them, not about me.” A corporate group on the Carolina Skiff one day, a single fly angler on the poling platform the next. Relaxed when it is time to be, dialed in when the fish show up. Everything is rigged before you step on the boat, the licenses are covered, and the start time moves with the tide, not the clock.

Kids are always welcome. Tanner rigs, baits and coaches, keeps the runs short and the action steady, and knows when a four-hour trip is the right call.
