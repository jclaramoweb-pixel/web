Accessibility

Last updated November 2026.

We want everyone to be able to find a trip, check prices and get in touch with Capt. Tanner. This website is built to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Pages use proper headings, landmarks and labels so they work with screen readers.
  • Every part of the site, including the menu, booking calendar and forms, can be used with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline.
  • Photos that carry information have text descriptions.
  • Text and buttons meet contrast guidelines, and pages work when zoomed to 200%.
  • Videos only play when you press play, and nothing on the site flashes.

Booking another way

If any part of the site is hard to use, you never need it to book. Call or text (352) 577-4084, seven days, or email captaintannermartin@gmail.com, and Tanner will set up your trip directly.

On the water

If someone in your group has a disability or needs a hand getting on and off the boat, tell Tanner when you book. He will pick the right boat and ramp and let you know honestly what each trip involves.

Tell us about a problem

If you find something on this site that does not work for you, please let us know by phone or email with the page and what happened. We will fix it.
