How a group trip works

One point of contact for the whole day. You give Tanner the headcount; he handles the boats, captains and timing.

1

Tell us the headcount and dates

Call, text or send the form below with how many people, the dates that work and what kind of day you want. Tanner replies the same day with boat count and a per-boat price.

2

Tanner sets the boats

His three boats plus partner captains he fishes with every week. Four anglers per boat inshore, six on a scalloping boat, three on the mud boat.

3

Everyone leaves together

One dock, one start time, boats working the same water. Groups can split by skill: beginners on the skiffs, the serious anglers on the fly boat.

4

Back at the dock together

Fish cleaned and bagged, group photo at the cleaning table, and lunch if you want us to arrange it.

Which trip for your group

Four of the six trips run well with several boats. Tarpon and fly are one or two anglers per boat and better as a side trip for the two people who want them.

Who books group trips

Company outings and client days

A half day on the water and lunch after beats a conference room. Everyone fishes, nobody needs experience, and the photos end up on the company Slack by noon.

Family reunions and multi-family trips

Scalloping in summer is the answer for a group of ten with a four-year-old and a grandfather in it. Shallow, warm water and a limit of scallops for the cookout.

Bachelor parties, birthdays and fishing clubs

Inshore in fall, mud boats in winter, tarpon for the two who care most. Tell us the occasion and the budget and we will build the day around it.

Group pricing is per boat

No group rate and no group markup. Each boat is the same price as a private trip, and the boats run together.

Example

12 people, inshore, 4 hours

Twelve people on a four-hour inshore outing is three boats at $600, so $1,800 for the group. Licenses, rods, tackle, bait, ice and fish cleaning are in that number. Lunch and the tip are not.

What’s included and what to bring

Included on every boat

  • Saltwater fishing license for everyone on board
  • Rods, reels, tackle and live or artificial bait
  • Ice and a cooler for your catch
  • Fish cleaning at the dock
  • Bottled water

Bring

  • Polarized sunglasses and a hat
  • Sunscreen and light long sleeves
  • Snacks, lunch and any drinks you want
  • A light rain jacket in summer
  • Cash for the 20% tip, per boat

Deposit

$100 per boat holds the date. Balance after the trip, one invoice for the group if you want it.

Cancellation
