What’s running when

The short version. Every report above has the detail for its month.

Fall

Sep to Nov

Redfish schooling on the flats, big trout staging, snook still on the mangroves, scallops through September.

Winter

Dec to Feb

Gator trout, sheepshead and black drum on structure, redfish on the oyster bars, duck hunts in the marsh.

Spring

Mar to May

Snook push back in, cobia on the outside, and the Homosassa tarpon migration starts in mid-April.

Summer

Jun to Aug

Tarpon through July, scalloping opens July 1, early mornings for reds and trout before the heat.
