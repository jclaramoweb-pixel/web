The migration, on the flats that made it famous

Every spring the tarpon push north along the Nature Coast and stack up on the clear, shallow flats off Homosassa, the same water where the fly-rod world records were set. Tanner leaves MacRae’s at 6 AM to be staked out on the travel lanes when the fish start rolling, and fishes through the morning tide until 2 PM.

This is a sight-fishing game: watching for rolling fish and daisy chains, waiting for the right shot and making the cast count. Fly anglers bring an 11 or 12-weight with a floating or intermediate line; spin anglers throw live bait or big plugs. Expect a few shots a day, and a fish that takes an hour to land.

Best for

  • Anglers chasing a first tarpon, on fly or spin
  • Experienced fly anglers who want a 150-pounder on the Homosassa flats
  • Anyone who has fished tarpon elsewhere and wants the original

Not the right trip if

  • You want steady action and a cooler of fish (see the Inshore Charter)
  • Kids or first-time anglers: this is a patience game
  • Your dates are outside mid-April to July

What to expect

Tarpon fishing is measured in shots, not fish. A good day is several good shots and one fish to the boat.

Tarpon

Mid-April to end of July • peak May and June

Eighty to 180 pounds, laid up or daisy-chaining on the flats in four to eight feet of clear water.

Fly tackle

11 or 12-weight

Floating or intermediate line, 60 to 80-pound bite tippet, black and purple or tan flies. Tanner has them tied.

Spin tackle

Heavy spinning gear

Live crabs, pinfish or big swimbaits on 50-pound braid. The higher-odds option for a first tarpon.

The weather window

Light wind, bright sun

Tarpon need calm, clear water to be seen. Tanner reschedules at no charge when the forecast is wrong.

Before you book

When is the best time?

May and June. The migration starts mid-April and tails off by the end of July. Book early; the peak weeks fill a year ahead.

How many fish will we catch?

Tarpon are counted in shots. A good day is several shots and a fish or two jumped; landing one is the trophy.

Fly or spin?

Both work. Spin tackle with live bait is the higher-odds route for a first tarpon; fly is the classic Homosassa game.

What if the wind is up?

You cannot sight-fish tarpon in chop. Tanner calls it the evening before and reschedules or refunds, your choice.
