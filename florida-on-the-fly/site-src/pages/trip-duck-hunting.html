Gulf-coast ducks without hauling a boat

When the fishing slows in late fall, Capt. Tanner swaps the push pole for the Prodigy surface drive and hunts the marshes, creeks and backwater ponds of the Nature Coast. Redheads and bluebills raft up on the open water, teal and the mixed bag work the marsh, and Tanner has been watching where they sit all week.

Hunts include the boat, decoys, blind and a captain who does the scouting. You bring your shotgun, shells, a Florida hunting license with state and federal duck stamps, and waders. Two hunters is standard; a third fits in the blind for $250 more.

Best for

  • Hunters who want Gulf-coast divers and puddle ducks without trailering a boat down
  • Visiting hunters who do not know the marsh
  • A cast-and-blast day: hunt the morning, fish the afternoon

Not the right trip if

  • Groups of four or more (three is the blind limit)
  • You need a dog on the hunt (ask first)
  • Your dates are outside mid-November to January

What you’ll hunt

The Nature Coast winters a mix of divers on the open water and puddle ducks in the marsh.

Redheads

December and January

Big rafts on the open bays once it gets cold up north. The Gulf-coast specialty.

Bluebills (scaup)

December and January

Rafted with the redheads, decoying hard on a windy morning.

Teal

November onward

Blue-wings and green-wings buzzing the marsh ponds at first light.

Mixed bag

All season

Pintail, wigeon, gadwall, mottled ducks and the occasional black duck, depending on the week.

Before you book

What license do I need?

A Florida hunting license with the state waterfowl permit and the federal duck stamp. Non-residents can buy a short-term license online.

Is a dog on the hunt?

Ask when you book. Retrieves are handled by the boat if there is no dog.

How many birds can we take?

Federal and Florida daily bag limits apply by species. Tanner knows the current limits and keeps the count.

Can we fish the same day?

Yes. Hunt the morning, then fish the afternoon on the Inshore Charter. Ask about cast-and-blast pricing.
