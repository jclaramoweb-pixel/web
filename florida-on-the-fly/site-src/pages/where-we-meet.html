Where we meet: Crystal River and Homosassa boat ramps

Every charter leaves from one of two docks. Pete’s Pier in Crystal River for inshore, fly, scalloping and mud boat trips; MacRae’s of Homosassa for tarpon. Addresses, parking and what to expect are below, plus the public ramps if you are trailering your own boat.

The two docks we leave from

Pete’s Pier, Crystal River

1 SW 1st Place, Crystal River, FL 34428
Inshore charter • Fly & light tackle • Scalloping • Mud boat trip

A city marina on Kings Bay, two minutes off US 19 in downtown Crystal River. Tanner will be at the dock with the boat in the water; look for the 23-foot Carolina Skiff or the Hells Bay.

Paid parking lot run by the City of Crystal River. Bring a card for the meter. Restrooms at the marina.

Arrive 15 minutes before your start time. Start times move with the tide and the season, so check your confirmation text the night before.

MacRae’s of Homosassa

5290 S Cherokee Way, Homosassa, FL 34448
Homosassa tarpon • Homosassa inshore and scalloping on request

A bait house, marina and motel on the Homosassa River in Old Homosassa, about 20 minutes south of Crystal River. All tarpon trips leave from here, and inshore or scalloping trips can when you are staying on this end.

Free parking for charter guests. Restrooms, ice and coffee at the bait house.

Same drill: arrive 15 minutes early, Tanner meets you at the dock. If you are staying at MacRae’s, you can walk down.

How the meet-up works

1

Pick your ramp when you book

Tell us where you are staying. Crystal River guests meet at Pete’s Pier, Homosassa guests at MacRae’s, and tarpon is always MacRae’s. The booking confirmation has the address and a map link.

2

Start time comes by text

Tanner sets the start to the tide, usually 7 AM in summer and 8 AM in fall and winter. You get a text the evening before with the exact time.

3

Bring what is on the list

Sunglasses, sunscreen, lunch and drinks. Licenses, rods, tackle, bait and ice are on the boat. The Rates page has the full included list.

4

Running late or lost?

Call or text {{PHONE}}. Both ramps are easy to miss the first time.
