Fishing like nowhere else in Florida

Trips start in Kings Bay, where manatees and dolphins are a common sight on the ride out and the sun comes up behind you. From there it's the mangrove creeks, oyster bars and grass flats of Crystal River and Homosassa, chasing the redfish, snook and trout the Nature Coast is known for.

Tanner fishes a corporate group on the Carolina Skiff one day and a single angler on the poling platform with a fly rod the next. Relaxed when it's time to be, dialed in when the fish show up.

Your captain

Meet Capt. Tanner Martin, Crystal River fishing guide

“I tell all my clients I try to make them feel like they are fishing with a friend.”

Tanner was born and raised on the Nature Coast. After playing Division 1 baseball at Stetson University and guiding part time through college, he went all in and has run Florida’s On The Fly since 2018. He’ll take a group of four-year-olds catching grunts one day and a fly angler hunting trophy redfish the next, and he offers fly casting lessons for anyone new to the long rod.
