Prices are per boat. Fishing licenses, rods, tackle, bait and ice are included on every fishing trip. Duck hunters bring their own shotgun, shells and stamps. A 20% tip for the captain is customary and not included.

How paying works

Three things to know before you book. Anything else, call Tanner at (352) 577-4084.

Per boat, not per person

Four anglers on a 4-hour inshore trip

Base price, 1 to 2 anglers
$600
3 or 4 anglers
+$100
Total for the boat
$700
Per angler
$175

Licenses, tackle, bait and ice are in that number. Lunch and the tip are not.

Deposit

$100 holds your date. The balance is due after the trip, cash or card. Cards carry a 3.5% processing fee.

Cancellation

Cancel more than 14 days out and the deposit comes back. Inside 14 days it is non-refundable, but it is credited to any future trip.

Weather

If Tanner calls the trip for weather, you get a full refund or a new date, your choice.

What’s included in every charter

The price on the table is the price you pay. No gear rental, no license run, no fuel surcharge.

Licenses

The boat’s saltwater license covers everyone on board.

Rods, tackle & bait

Spin, fly and light tackle rigged before you arrive. Live or artificial bait.

Scalloping gear

Masks, snorkels, fins and mesh bags in season.

Ice & cleaning

A cooler for your catch and cleaning at the dock.
