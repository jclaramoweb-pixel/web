Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026.

Florida’s On The Fly Charters (“we”, “us”) runs this website to tell people about our fishing charters and to help them book a trip. This page explains what information we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. When you use the contact or group quote form, or email, call or text us, we receive what you give us: usually your name, phone number, email, trip dates, group size and message.
  • Booking information. Online bookings and deposits are handled by our booking provider. They collect the details needed to hold your date and take payment under their own privacy policy. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic site data. Our web host keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and to keep the site running.

How we use it

We use your information only to answer your questions, plan and run your trip, send you trip details such as start time and meeting place, and keep records of bookings and payments. We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not add you to a marketing list unless you ask to be on one.

Who we share it with

Only the services we need to run the business: our booking and payment provider, our email and form provider, and our web host. If your group needs more than one boat, we share names and the trip plan with the partner captain running the second boat. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Embedded content

Some pages include YouTube videos and links to Google Maps. Videos only load from YouTube when you press play, and those services follow their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Call or text (352) 577-4084 or email captaintannermartin@gmail.com.

Children

Kids are welcome on our boats, but this website is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information online.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
