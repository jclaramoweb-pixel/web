Fishing Homosassa, FL

Homosassa is a spring-fed river town 20 minutes south of Crystal River, and for a few months every year it is the most famous tarpon water in the world. The rest of the year it is some of the best inshore fishing on the Gulf coast: clear flats, oyster bars, mangrove creeks and the Chassahowitzka backcountry to the south, with a fraction of the boats you will see farther down the coast.

Capt. Tanner grew up fishing both rivers and runs trips out of MacRae’s of Homosassa whenever the fishing or your lodging puts you here. Same boats, same prices, same booking calendar as our Crystal River charters.

The Homosassa water

Three kinds of water inside a 20-minute run of the dock. Read Where we fish for the whole coast.

The outside flats

Where the river meets the Gulf the bottom goes hard and the water goes clear, and wide flats run for miles in five to eight feet. This is the tarpon water from mid-April into July, when strings of 100-pound fish come up the coast. Billy Pate and Jim Holland Jr. set the fly-rod world records here. Outside the migration, the same flats hold trout, cobia and scallops.

The river mouth and the bars

The lower Homosassa River is lined with old fish camps and opens onto a maze of oyster bars, grass and mangrove islands. Redfish and trout live here most of the year, snook stack up in the creek mouths from spring through fall, and sheepshead and black drum take over the rock in winter. A four-hour inshore trip rarely has to run more than 20 minutes.

The Chassahowitzka backcountry

South of the river the coast turns into the Chassahowitzka National Wildlife Refuge: salt marsh, tidal creeks and shallow bays with no houses on them. It is fly and light tackle water on the skiff, mud boat water on the lowest winter tides, and where the duck hunts go from November to January.

When to fish Homosassa

The short version. The fishing reports have the detail for this month.

Spring

Mar to May

Snook move back out of the river, cobia show on the outside rock, and the first tarpon arrive in mid-April. May is peak tarpon.

Summer

Jun to Aug

Tarpon through July, then scallops. Reds and trout at first light before the heat and the afternoon storms.

Fall

Sep to Nov

Scallops through September 24. Then the best inshore fishing of the year: schooling redfish, big trout, snook on the mangroves.

Winter

Dec to Feb

Gator trout, sheepshead and black drum on the rock, reds in the mud creeks on low tides. Duck season in the Chassahowitzka marsh.
