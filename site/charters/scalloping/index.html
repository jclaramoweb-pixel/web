“Fish” by hand

Crystal River and Homosassa are the scalloping capital of Florida. From the July opener through September, Capt. Tanner runs you out to the grass flats where the water is waist to chest deep and warm as a bath, hands everyone a mask, snorkel and mesh bag, and you swim the grass picking scallops off the bottom.

It is the easiest day on the water a family can have. Kids as young as four do it with a life jacket, grandparents do it, and the boat is right there the whole time. On a good day you limit out in a couple of hours and Tanner cleans the catch at the dock so you can cook it that night.

Best for

  • Families with kids, the younger the better
  • Groups of friends who want a day in the water, not just on it
  • Anyone visiting Crystal River in summer who has never scalloped
  • Non-anglers in a fishing family

Not the right trip if

  • You want to fish (ask about a fish-and-scallop combo instead)
  • Your dates are outside July to September
  • Nobody in the group wants to get in the water

Scalloping in Homosassa, FL: what you need to know

Scalloping is simple. These are the four things everyone asks.

Bay scallops

Season opens July 1, runs into September

Small, sweet and plentiful on the grass flats in four to six feet of water. You spot the blue eyes and pick them up.

Limits

2 gallons whole per person • 10 per boat

Florida limits are generous. A good day fills everyone’s bag by lunch.

Gear on the boat

Masks, snorkels, fins, mesh bags

Everything to get in the water is provided, plus the saltwater license that covers everyone on board.

Swimming

Waist to chest deep

Life jackets for the kids, a dive flag on the boat and Tanner within shouting distance the whole time.

Before you book

Do I need a license?

No. The boat’s saltwater license covers everyone on board for scalloping.

Can young kids do it?

Yes. Kids as young as four scallop in a life jacket with a parent. The water is shallow and warm.

Do I need to be a strong swimmer?

No. You float over the grass in waist to chest-deep water and the boat is always close.

Can we fish too?

Ask Tanner about a fish-and-scallop combo morning. Scallop season overlaps good inshore fishing.
